Help with Chido on iPhone, iPad and Android

What Chido is

Chido is an app for learning the Spanish spoken in Mexico: the most common words first, with everyday phrases, example sentences and reviews timed so you remember them.

Your progress

Progress is saved only on your phone. Reset all progress in Settings erases it; deleting the app does too. There is no account to recover, so progress cannot be moved to a new phone unless your phone's own backup restores it.

Speaking practice

If Speaking does not hear you, check that Chido is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition in your phone's settings, and that Spanish is available for dictation on your phone. You can switch Speaking off in Chido's Settings.
